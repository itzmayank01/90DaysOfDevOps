# Task 6: Correlate Metrics and Logs in Grafana

## Documentation: How does having metrics and logs in the same tool (Grafana) help during incident response compared to checking separate systems?

Having metrics and logs consolidated in a single platform like Grafana offers several crucial advantages during an incident response (like a production outage or performance degradation), compared to the traditional approach of switching between separate monitoring tools and log aggregators.

### 1. Rapid Context Switching (The "What" vs. the "Why")
- **Metrics** tell you *what* is broken (e.g., "CPU utilization just spiked to 100%").
- **Logs** tell you *why* it is broken (e.g., "A specific user query is entering an infinite loop").
- **Unified Tool:** In Grafana, you can use the Explore split view to look at both side-by-side. When you identify an anomaly in a metric graph, you can instantly see the exact logs generated by the system at that precise moment without changing tabs or logging into another system.

### 2. Time Synchronization
When using disparate tools, incident responders often waste critical minutes trying to manually align the timestamps of a metric spike in Prometheus with the timestamps of logs in Kibana or CloudWatch.
- **Unified Tool:** Grafana automatically synchronizes the time range across panels. Clicking on a CPU spike on the left panel (Prometheus) will automatically restrict the log query on the right panel (Loki) to that exact time range, ensuring you are looking at the relevant logs instantly.

### 3. Shared Metadata and Labels
- **Unified Tool:** Tools like Prometheus and Loki use the exact same labeling system (e.g., `job="docker"`, `container_name="notes-app"`). You can filter metrics and logs using the exact same dimensions. This means you don't have to translate a server IP from your metrics tool into a generic hostname search in your logging tool.

### 4. Reduced Cognitive Load and MTTR
During an incident, stress is high. Having to remember queries and login credentials for multiple systems increases cognitive load.
- **Unified Tool:** A single pane of glass keeps responders focused. The faster you can trace a symptom (metric anomaly) to its root cause (error log), the lower your Mean Time To Resolution (MTTR) will be.

---

### Instructions for setting up the Split View (As requested in the lab):
1. Navigate to **Explore** (compass icon on the left sidebar in Grafana: `http://localhost:3000`).
2. Click the **Split** button near the top right to open two side-by-side panels.
3. **Left Panel (Metrics):**
   - Data source: `Prometheus`
   - Query: `rate(container_cpu_usage_seconds_total{name="notes-app"}[5m])`
4. **Right Panel (Logs):**
   - Data source: `Loki`
   - Query: `{container_name="notes-app"}`
5. Once configured, try clicking on a specific peak in the Prometheus graph on the left. Notice how the Loki logs on the right automatically adjust to that exact timeframe.
